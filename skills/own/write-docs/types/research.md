# Research note

A research note answers one question about the outside world with sources, and says what it means for us.

Budget: 800 words typical, 1,500 max (our number). Raw data (tables, logs, CSV) lives outside the note and is linked.

## Sections

1. **Question and answer**: the question in one line, the answer in three sentences.
2. **Findings**: a table of facts, each with a link and the date read; anything not read is marked **unverified**.
3. **Fit**: what we take, what we do not, and why.
4. **Proposal**: the smallest next step, or none.

## Keep it short

- A quote is at most two lines; link the rest.
- Options with a decision go to an ADR; the note keeps the evidence.
