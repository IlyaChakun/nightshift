# ADR (architecture decision record)

An ADR records one decision: the context, the options, the choice and its consequences. It is written once and superseded, not edited, when the decision changes.

Budget: 400 words typical, 1,000 max. Source: "The whole document should be one or two pages long" ([Nygard, Documenting Architecture Decisions](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions), read 2026-11-26); MADR asks for the context "in free form using two to three sentences" ([MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/), read 2026-11-26).

## Sections (MADR)

1. **Context and problem statement**: two or three sentences.
2. **Considered options**: at least two, one line each.
3. **Decision outcome**: the chosen option and the reason, then consequences (good, bad).
4. Optional, only when they change the reader's view: decision drivers, pros and cons per option, confirmation (how we check it holds).

## Keep it short

- One decision per ADR. A second decision is a second ADR.
- Research data goes to a research note; the ADR links it and keeps the conclusion.
- Over the max: move the option comparison to the research note.
